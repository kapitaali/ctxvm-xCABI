## 1. Mechanism, measured before the fix

DSH publishes `SessionEvent = {type, seq, time, data, surfaceOp?, sourceEventSeqs?}`
(`packages/core/session/src/types.ts:506-529`). A real event from the earlier headless
capture was `{"type":"request/header","seq":12,…,"data":{"header":{"config":{"provider":"opencode","model":"hy4-flash-free"}}}}`.
The adapter read `event.header`, `event.message`, `event.arguments`, `event.callId` and
`event.surfaceOp` directly (`RR-DSHEVENT-20261116` §2), so every payload read saw
`undefined`; the flattened unit mocks kept the suite green. Consequences on record:
a 3-event skeleton with `provider: dsh` / `model: unknown` (`RR-DSHTRACE-20261116`), and
915 projected events folding to one page (`RR-DSHPROJ-20261116`).

Files before edit were snapshotted to `/tmp/dsh-lane-plana/2026-11-16/` (five files,
sha256s in that directory). No file was deleted or moved.

## 2. What changed (all inside `packages/deepseek/**`)

- **New `src/event-data.ts`**: `eventData(event)` merges `data` payload fields with
  envelope metadata (`type`, `seq`, `time`, `surfaceOp`, `sourceEventSeqs`, `ignorable`),
  plus small `asRecord` / `asString` / `asNumberArray` narrowing helpers. One helper,
  every read site.
- **`src/event-map.ts`**: all reads go through the helper; `tool/call`, `tool/result`,
  `user/message`, `system/message`, `assistant/message` (including `usage`) and
  `request/header` now see the real payload. A message-less `tool/result` passes an
  empty body to `tracer.page`, keeping the tracer total (no repeat of RR-DSHPROJ).
- **`src/page-table.ts`**: the fold reads the merged event; `seq` comes from the
  envelope, `shadowedSeqs`/`surfaceOp` from the right layer. First-declare-wins,
  stub/eviction rules and schema factory unchanged.
- **`src/tracer.ts`** + **`src/index.ts`**: `@ctxvm/node` fixes `session.open` identity
  at `createTrace` time, but `request/header` is committed **after** system/user
  events in a real DSH session. `ensureTracer` now buffers pre-header events and
  replays them in order when the header arrives, using its real provider/model;
  `finalizeTracer` creates fail-safe placeholders at disposal for sessions with no
  header, so the trace is still written.
- **Tests**: mocks converted to `{type, seq, time, data}` fixtures. Required assertions
  added: a leading `request/header` yields real provider/model in `session.open`, and
  a `tool/result` yields at least one `page.declare`. Also pinned: buffered pages are
  replayed with the late identity, provider usage survives the envelope, and the
  no-message resume regression stays green.
