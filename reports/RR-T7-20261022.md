## Discoveries

1. **`session.context` is primary-only by construction** — compaction/generate/title
   prepare paths trigger their own hook keys (`model-request.ts` L404–407). The
   "kind-filter" risk assumed in planning dissolves in OpenCode v2.1.x.
2. **No bus-event surface for plugins**: `@opencode/schema/plugin` exports only the
   `plugin.updated` lifecycle event. `identifier_scan` therefore anchors on the
   `aisdk.language` pass-through tap (the only object-returning hook — an explicit,
   optional enablement) or on a render-window scan fallback (one-turn lag, T3 dedupe).
3. **Post-hook mutation exists upstream:** `tool.ts` applies
   `normalizeImages(normalizeContent(...))` after the `execute.after` trigger and the
   step loop applies `toolOutput.truncate(outcome)` — mirrored content can exceed what
   the model finally sees (flagged approximation; render-window re-derivation is the
   candidate fix, RFC-1 material).
4. **Vercel AI SDK has approval/denial first-class parts** (`tool-error`,
   `tool-output-denied`, approval request/response) and **first-party harness adapters**
   in the same tree (`@ai-sdk/harness-opencode` et al.) — supports the M2 wedge and
   makes payload-typed anchoring (not shape copying) the right isolation choice.
5. **T6 continuity:** adapters replace the `vm.call` front door with hook registration,
   the recording contract (identity minting, §4 matrix, resident re-call ledger rows)
   stays identical — one matrix, two front doors.
