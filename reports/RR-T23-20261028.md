# RR — T23 — export dir-hygiene guard: foreign-slice protection at export time

**Card:** T23 — close the m7-recorded engine wart: a second `export` into the
same `--output` dir at a DIFFERENT turn silently overwrites the shared
`--odps` trio (`manifest.yaml`, `llms.txt`, `checksums.sha256` — names carry
no turn) and orphans the older slice's checksum coverage. Proposal
`reports/T23-PROPOSAL-20261028.md`.

**Rulings on record (2026-10-28, §7):** the operator accepted ALL
recommendations — (1) foreign-slice collision **BLOCKS with exit 2 unless
`--force`**; (2) same-turn re-export = clean silent pass (the m6/m7 restore

when forced; (4) the ai-sdk double-pause ride-along test included; (5) one

## What was done

1. **`rust/crates/ctxvm-conformance/src/cli.rs`** — the guard inside
   `export_core` (the single exit/serialization site; ctxvm-py inherits the
   law through the binary — byte-identity contract — with no py-side change;
   its tests all use fresh dirs). After the effective turn is derived and
   BEFORE any write: scan the target dir (missing/unreadable → clean pass)
   for turn-named artifacts whose NUMERIC turn differs (`trace-N.jsonl[.zst]`,
   `page-table-N.json` — `trace-02` compares equal to turn 2) and for a
   `manifest.yaml` whose `sliceTurn` differs (light parse of the
   module-generated manifest; absent/unparseable → not foreign).
   - No foreign artifacts → unchanged behavior, no `dirHygiene` field.
   - Foreign present, no `--force` → `CliOutcome::fail(2, …)` with a reason
     that names the foreign artifacts and the remedy — **the target dir is
     left byte-untouched** (the guard runs before `create_dir_all`/writes).
   - Foreign present with `--force` → proceeds; a warning per foreign
     artifact (`dir hygiene forced: …`) rides the existing warnings law, and
     the report gains `dirHygiene: {"status":"forced","foreign":[…]}`.
   - Doc block on `export_core` records the law.

2. **`rust/crates/ctxvm-conformance/src/bin/export.rs`** — `--force` argv

## Disclosed

- **The mirrored pause `verified` default rides through the guard:** the
  reference pause law is `verified` = "the replay counterproof passed" — a
  blocked export produces NO package, so the counterproof never runs and
  `verified` keeps its default `true` while `exit_code=2` + the stderr reason
  are the loud failure. The ride-along test pins exactly this shape (an
  initial draft asserted `verified=false` — corrected to the mirrored law,
  no module change; mirroring ≠ redesign).
- **Forced runs do not delete the older slice** — it survives without
  checksum coverage; the resume-side `sliceTurn` cross-check (UC1-VERIFY-CHECKS)
  fail-closes such dirs loudly. Both facts asserted in the rust forced test.
- **Scope:** trio leftovers without a parseable manifest, and artifacts
  outside the turn-name patterns, are not detected (documented; the m7-shape
  collision is fully covered). ctxvm-py and the opencode/hermes/node lanes
  are untouched; gate-3's round-trip and the TS golden tests all export into
  fresh dirs and are byte-identical.
