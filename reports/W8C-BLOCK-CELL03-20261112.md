## 1. The one question, and the claim ceiling

Does the cost direction measured on the free tier (paged arm: 4.73× and 4.77× the control's uncached
input) hold under a paid provider's real billing at the final 20-prompt shape?

The result may support only: "one pair, `openai/gpt-6-luna`, 20 prompts, budget 60,000: the paged arm
cost <x> times the control in USD (provider `debited_usd`), with the request held at <ratio> of the
control's." It supports no saving (law 7), no accuracy claim beyond a census task, no page-level claim,
no generalisation beyond this subject, shape and budget. After this pair, no further spend until the
engine changes (owner ruling 2026-11-12).

## 5. Attribution, copied at run time

Arm, profile, budget, subject, selection and call counts are read from the run's own output and written
into the record as read — never re-read from a capture afterwards. Second witnesses: `sessions.profile_name`
in each profile's DB, and the profile's own `config.yaml`.

## 6. Cost

`debited_usd` delta per key, before and after, one key per arm; never from `providerUsage`, the session
store or a hand ledger. Granularity is the provider's daily export; both arms run inside one UTC day
(late-evening Finnish time is the next UTC day).

## 7. Deviation handling

A mismatch whose cause is identified as a specification error (our unit, our bracket, our recipe) is a
RECORDED DEVIATION: recorded, explained, corrected, and the cell stands. A mismatch with an unexplained
cause is an INSTRUMENT-BUG: stop, do not book. A CEILING or a NO-OP is a booked outcome.
