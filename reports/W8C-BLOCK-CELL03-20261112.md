input) hold under a paid provider's real billing at the final 20-prompt shape?

cost <x> times the control in USD (provider `debited_usd`), with the request held at <ratio> of the
control's." It supports no saving (law 7), no accuracy claim beyond a census task, no page-level claim,
no generalisation beyond this subject, shape and budget. After this pair, no further spend until the
engine changes (owner ruling 2026-11-12).

## 6. Cost — restated for the nous provider, before any call

The draft assumed one provider key per arm and a per-key `debited_usd` export. **That is not this
provider.** Both arms ride the owner's nous gateway, so the account's bill cannot be split by arm.

- **Per-arm cost is receipt-priced**: `call.meta.providerUsage` summed per arm, multiplied by the
  published Luna rates (input $0.10-0.13/1M, cached input $0.01/1M, output $0.50/1M). It is an
  estimate from tokens the provider itself reported, not a billed figure, and it is labelled as such.
- **The pair's billed figure is the account's delta** for the day, before minus after, read from the
  provider's export. It carries no per-arm split and includes any other paid session in the day, so
  the run is the only paid activity in it and that is stated in the card.
- **Cache writes are not reported** by this provider's usage records (`inputTokens`,
  `outputTokens`, `cachedInputTokens` only), so P8/P9 are stated as **cache-read** share and no
  cache-write claim is made. Granularity is the daily export; both arms run inside one UTC day
  (late-evening Finnish time is the next UTC day).

## 7. Deviation handling

A mismatch whose cause is identified as a specification error (our unit, our bracket, our recipe) is a
RECORDED DEVIATION: recorded, explained, corrected, and the cell stands. A mismatch with an unexplained
cause is an INSTRUMENT-BUG: stop, do not book. A CEILING or a NO-OP is a booked outcome.
