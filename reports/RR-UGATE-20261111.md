ERRATUM (2026-11-11, appended; nothing above is rewritten): the blast-radius framing in the P10
analysis (this card's §3 and the packet's Evidence 4) OVERSTATES the marginal cost of the additive-field
option, and the reviewer's P10 answer corrects it. Corrected: a strict v0 consumer already rejects a
v0.1 trace at its `session.open` line — measured: the sealed v0 validator rejects the umpire ab-demo
ledger with 4 failures (lines 1, 3, 4, 6: the `schemaVersion` line and the three `intentional_rerun`
fault rows) — so an added `page.declare` field does NOT newly break v0-only readers of v0.1 data. The
MARGINAL costs of that option are (i) every v0.1-aware consumer must be updated (the strict allowlist
rejects an unknown field), (ii) two sources of identity per line, and (iii) a consistency rule between
them. Also: `schemaVersion` is declared on the `session.open` line ONLY and is optional
(`spec/rfc-0.1/RFC-0.1-xCABI.md:8`), so the "if every v0.1 line carried schemaVersion" mitigation does
not apply. The reviewer's conclusion that the additive field is out is nevertheless RIGHT, for a
stronger reason than the one recorded above: the asymmetry lives in the SEALED v0 schema itself
(`spec/rfc-0/x-cabi.schema-v0.json`: `page.declare.page` -> `pageRef{id: pageId{logicalRef,contentHash},
kind, tokens}` while `page.render`, `page.evict`, `page.fault`, `page.pin`, `page.unpin` -> `pageId`),
RFC-0.1 leaves it untouched, and sealed v0 traces exist that will not change - so every consumer of
them must keep the type-dispatch rule forever, and a field would add a THIRD shape without removing
either old one. The adopted ruling is (b) + (a): ONE RFC-0.1 compatibility-note line, no field, with a
conformance vector to make the rule testable. Full record and the note's landing route (a new file
under `spec/rfc-0.1/`, D-numbered): `reports/RR-P10ID-20261111.md`.
