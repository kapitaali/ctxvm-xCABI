# RR-G4GO-20261118 — owner rules G4 GO, narrowly scoped

Owner decision 2026-11-18, verbatim:

> GO the sidecar assertion record with an explicit "unknown" consumer rule and a declaration-only enum; refuse to fold in a keyed emitter, wire field, verifier claim, or release authorization.

This closes G4's *Owner: go/defer* line with **go** and fixes the scope of the first implementation card.

## Authorized scope

- A **sidecar assertion record** beside the artifact, following `design/redaction-block.md` (sidecar-first; no
  wire change). Its required shape is the stage list — capture / flush / export entries with `policyId`,
  `ruleSetVersion`, `stage`, `replacedFieldCount`.
- An **explicit consumer rule**: absence of the record means **"unknown"**, never "none".
- A **declaration-only enum**: `raw | keyed-hashed | dropped`, with `raw` as the shipped declared value. Declaring
  a value is not emitting it.

## Explicitly refused — not in this card

- No **keyed-hash emitter**. Only the enum declaration; the future owner-gated emitting card and its golden-vector
  precondition remain untouched.
- No **wire field**: no RFC-0.1 additive field, no five-touchpoint card, no v0.2 design note.
- No **verifier claim**: the record stays an assertion by the writer. The existing scanner remains a pre-drop lint;
  no tamper-evident chain, no seeded-canary claim, and no "verified"/"redacted" wording beyond the `w8-dq-v1`
  pattern-match statement.
- No **release authorization**: D17 remains held until the owner judges there is enough data. No registry action,
  package bump, catalog submission, or push.

**Open but not folded in:** the `stubText`/`reason` bounded-vocabulary and pre-drop-validation mitigations stay as
the card's explicit UNKNOWN; this ruling does not decide them. The separate consent/release plugin card (first-run
notice minimum, opt-in stronger) stays bound at D6/D17 and owner-routed.
