## What the instrument change adds to the read

Cell-01 could not distinguish "the policy held the budget" from "nothing happened". Cell-02 can,
because `turn.render.evictedTokens` now carries the engine's own count:

```
per turn        evictedTokens by turn; a non-zero value first at turn 14 or later
                cumulative 5,000-20,000 tokens over the session
control arm     turn.render.evictedTokens == 0 on every turn, and "control selects nothing" passes
```

A **zero** on the paged arm at turn 14 or later is the falsifier, and it is now decidable. Either the
floor is binding (evictions appear at the predicted volume) or the instrument still lies (predicted
volume missed by more than the stated range) — those are different outcomes and must not be merged.

## Attribution, copied at run time

The arm, profile, budget and selection counts are read from the run's own output and written into the
record as they are read — never re-read from a capture afterwards, because a restart re-flushes
captures with then-current values and a later reader can see another session's arm.
