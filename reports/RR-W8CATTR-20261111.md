That session ran in the **paged** profile — it appears **20×** in
`~/.hermes/profiles/w8cn-paged/logs/agent.log` and **0×** in the naive profile's — and its sidecar
attests the **control** arm's identity: profile `default`, arm `control`, `engineSetting compressor`,

**The mechanism (consistent with every observation):** the arm fields still live in the tracer's
**process-global** state (this lane's `reports/RR-W8BARMSTATE-20261111.md`, item 1 — *key the arm fields
by session id* — still open), and they are read **at flush time**. The probe read the sidecar at
~20:04, right after that session's flush, when the global values were still the paged arm's ✓ — which is
why its verdict was right. At **20:05:44** — a gateway restart's re-flush of every tracer in the
process — **all six** captures in the trace directory were rewritten with the *then-current* global
values (the default profile's), so each session's sidecar now carries the **same** arm attribution, and
the paged session's carries the control arm's.

Two consequences, both actionable:

1. **The attribution evidence must be read at run time and copied into the record.** Their LOG row does
   exactly that ✓ — the checkpoint-A numbers live in the ledger, not in the capture. But any later
   reading of a capture's `plugin` block (by the block's author at step 5, by a verifier days later, by
   the pair's own post-hoc check) can see **another session's arm** and conclude VOID or mis-attribution
   — a false negative, on a cell that was correctly attributed at the time.
2. **The still-open fix is now observably load-bearing.** Until the arm fields are keyed per session,
   a capture's arm identity is not a property of the capture's session, and the trace directory's
   captures all carry the last-flushed session's identity after any restart. The pair (step 6) is the
   experiment whose evidence this is; the block (step 5) is written from the diagnostic's capture.

**What this lane does not claim:** that the checkpoint-A verdict was wrong (it was right, read at the
right moment), that the pair cannot be attributed (it can — read at run time, copied into the record),
or that the 0.12.0 instrument is broken (it is not). The finding is about **when** the attribution is
true, and about a failure mode that would silently invalidate a correct cell.
