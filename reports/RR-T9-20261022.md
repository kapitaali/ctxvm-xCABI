## RR-T7's two open questions — RESOLVED from the pinned source

1. **`toolOutput.truncate` reach-through — CONFIRMED.** `runner/step.ts` (pinned SHA):
   `executeTool(…) ⇒ Effect.flatMap(toolOutput.truncate) ⇒ publisher.toolExecution(...)`.
   The `tool.execute.after` hook fires inside `executeTool`, i.e. ALWAYS before
   truncation; the step loop truncates afterwards (`core/tool-output.ts`:
   `MAX_LINES = 2_000`, `MAX_BYTES = 50 KiB`; over-limit results get a
   `[showing lines 1-N of M; full output saved to <file>]` marker and
   `metadata.truncated = true` + `outputPath`). Also image normalization applies
   post-hook (`tool.ts`), exactly as T7 risk 1 suggested.
   **Impact:** the page body (hook-time content) overstates what the transcript
   finally held for oversized results; identity is unaffected (deterministic
   full-content hash — repeated calls hash identically). v0 keeps the approximation
   (T3 posture); the plugin stays honest by NOT attempting byte-exact re-derivation.
   RFC-1: truncated-variant pages or next-window re-derivation.
2. **Provider-executed tools — CONFIRMED.** `runner/step.ts`: the stream subscription
   runs `if (event.type !== "tool-call" || event.providerExecuted) return` before
   pushing the `executeTool` fiber — provider-executed calls never enter
   `executeTool`, so no `tool.execute.before/after` hooks fire and no pages declare.
   Their outputs still enter the transcript via the provider stream. v0 documents
   the gap; render-window declaration is the RFC-1 mitigation (as T7 flagged).

Additional source-verified facts adopted by the implementation: `session.context`
fires only for `kind:"primary"` by construction (compaction/generate/title have
their own hooks — no kind filtering needed), and only `execute.before` may fail
upstream (`ToolFailures` in `effect/tool.ts`), which the adapter never does.

## Timing mapping (RFC-0 §5 conformance)

`session.context` = one model request = one turn. Inside the handler, scan runs
BEFORE the render call: the texts entering the window were produced during the
previous turn's lifecycle → `identifier_scan` faults attribute to that turn; the
tracer's per-page·source dedupe absorbs the re-walk across later windows. Tool hooks
fire mid-turn → declares land for the next turn's reveal; `tool_recall` faults carry
turn t while the resident page stays untouched (the ledger row IS the metric).

Deliberate omission: `aisdk.language` tap is NOT used (T7 risk 5) — the history
fallback keeps every surface read-only; a fixture demonstrates the scan channel
working with zero mutable-object exposure.
