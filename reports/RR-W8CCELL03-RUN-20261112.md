**The P8/P9 deviation is a specification error, and it is identified as such rather than read as an
instrument fault** (ratified rule item 3). Both arms report `inputTokens: 60` and a cache-read share of
100 % because this provider reports `inputTokens` as the **uncached remainder** only, with the reusable
prefix in `cachedInputTokens`; a share computed as cached ÷ (input + cached) is 100 % by construction.
No cache-write bucket is reported at all. The quantity is uninformative under this provider.

figures, because the write term is invisible in the receipts. The per-arm figures above are the
gateway's `sessions.estimated_cost_usd`; their formula is **UNVERIFIED**. The billed figure is
