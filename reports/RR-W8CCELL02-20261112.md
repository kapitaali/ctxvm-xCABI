## 2. The finding: the predicted volume is stale by an order of magnitude, and the block's own preamble
## already contradicts it

The block predicts:

```
predicted evictions   first at turn 14 or later; volume 5-20 k tokens
```

and justifies it as *"cell-01's numbers frozen unchanged … because they were committed before any result
existed. … nothing measured since contradicts them."*

**That last clause does not hold.** Cell-01's own run measured the eviction volume — indirectly, as the
difference between the two arms' residency series (the control arm's residency *is* the request the paged
arm would have sent), and the block's own preamble cites that measurement ("the paged request stopped

- **the first eviction is at turn 4**, not turn 14 ✓ (ten turns earlier than the prediction);
- **the session total is 185,303 tokens**, not 5–20 k ✓ — **9× to 37× the block's upper bound**;
- the peak is **42,186 tokens at turn 19** ✓.

**The operational consequence, which is why this is worth saying before the run and not after:** if the
paged arm's `turn.render.evictedTokens` in cell-02 lands where the measurement says it must (~185 k over
the session), then the block's stated range "5–20 k" is **missed by an order of magnitude** → by the
block's own verdict line (*"a mismatch means the instrument is wrong"*) the run would be classified
**INSTRUMENT-BUG** and **not booked** — even though the instrument is now correct and the treatment is
real. That would send the lane to re-fix a working instrument and would discard the first
trace-witnessable cell in the card. The reverse is harmless: a run at 5–20 k would mean the floor is
binding (the other branch of the falsifier) ✓ — which is exactly why the two branches must be separated.

## 3. Two honest fixes (their choice; the discipline allows either)

1. **State the measured prediction** — evictions from turn ~4 (479 tokens) rising to ~42 k at turn 19, the
   session total ~185 k, with the range bracket around the *measurement* (e.g. 150–220 k), and the
   three-way outcome rule: *within the range → the floor is not binding and the policy acts as predicted;
   below it → the floor is binding; above it → the instrument lies*. Then the verifier's check becomes a
   **number to match**, not a sign ✓ (this lane's `RR-W8CNEXT` §2).
2. **Keep the conservative range but state it as a THRESHOLD, not a prediction**: "the falsifier is
   `evictedTokens == 0` on the paged arm at turn 14 or later; the *expected* volume is ~185 k (the
   measured arms' difference), and a total below 20 k would mean the floor is binding rather than that the
   instrument lies." The block's own sentence already separates those two outcomes ("those are different
   outcomes and must not be merged") ✓ — so the *intent* is right and only the number needs to stop
   contradicting the measurement beside it.

**On the freeze discipline itself** — no objection to keeping a *prediction* unchanged within a cell, and
no objection to a new cell carrying a better-founded prediction: the books' rule is that the prediction
must be **committed before the run** ✓ (it is), and that a reopened question is a **fresh
preregistration** ✓ (cell-02 is one). What the rule forbids is fitting the prediction to the outcome
*after* seeing it — and here the outcome is already in hand, so the number must reflect it, with the
reason recorded (the measurement that justifies it) ✓.
