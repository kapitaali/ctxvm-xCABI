contextvar home (the old path ✓✓) would resolve to nothing ✓✓ → "compressor" ✓✓ → the early return ✓✓ →

`reports/RR-W8CATTR-20261111.md` ✓✓ (the arm is resolved *two* ways ✓✓, and they can disagree ✓✓).

⇒ **⇒ The fix is ours** ✓✓: the plugin already resolves the policy at **hook** time ✓✓ — it should
**pass that resolution (or the home ✓✓) into the engine's per-call resolution** ✓✓, instead of the
engine re-resolving from a contextvar that may not travel ✓✓.

⇒ **⇒ Two consequences for the two ways forward** ✓✓:
