# RR-H11FLOOR-20261118 — the free measurement that decides H12's budget: 24 k acts and can win, 60 k acts and cannot

Owner instruction: "$1 cap. but do the other measurements first." This is that measurement — no model call, no
spend. The W8C captures that would have given it directly are **not in the pool any more** (retention pruned
them; no run bundle holds them either), so the floor was measured **from the engine itself** on the real T-A v2
fixture bytes. Method and its one limitation are stated below.

## 1. Method

Rebuild the T-A v2 request from `experiments/w8b/ws-naive/fixture/part-*.txt` (20 parts, one
assistant-tool-call + tool-result pair each, plus a system prompt and one user turn), then run
`select_context` on a fresh engine at each budget and read the H9 plan block. Two system-prompt sizes: **300
tokens** (the terse profile `design/w8-run-plan.md` asks for) and **5,483** (the size measured on a real W8
capture). Host-ABC stub installed the way the suite installs it, so this is the shipped engine, not a copy.

## 2. The measurement — resident 80,959 tokens of fixture

| budget | floor (300-tok system) | evicted | outgoing | floor (5,483-tok system) | evicted | outgoing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **24,000** | **16,714** | 59,796 | **21,163** | 21,897 | 63,909 | 22,233 |
| 30,000 | 16,714 | 51,825 | 29,134 | 21,897 | 59,796 | 26,346 |
| 40,000 | 16,714 | 43,933 | 37,026 | 21,897 | 47,989 | 38,153 |
| 60,000 | 16,714 | 23,971 | 56,988 | 21,897 | 27,715 | 58,427 |
| 90,000 | 16,714 | 0 | 80,959 | 21,897 | 0 | 86,142 |

Two facts fall out, and the second one is the decision:

1. **At 24,000 the arm acts.** Floor 16,714 < 24,000 < resident 80,959, so pages are evicted and the outgoing
   request lands at **21,163** — under the budget and under H12's acceptance target of 24,000.
2. **At 60,000 the arm acts and cannot win.** Outgoing 56,988 sits **above the design's break-even prefix of
   57,250 at 14 calls** — a perfectly cached paged arm of that shape is still a loss.

## 3. Price units at the measured prefixes (naive = 13,167 units, the design's own figure)

| calls | 24 k arm (P = 21,163) | 60 k arm (P = 56,988) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 2,963 = **22.5 %** of naive | 7,978 = 60.6 % |
| 8 | 3,598 = **27.3 %** | 9,688 = 73.6 % |
| 14 | 4,867 = **37.0 %** | 13,107 = 99.5 % |

So the regime the design predicted (42 % of naive at 24 k) is not only reachable — with a terse system prompt
it is slightly better. At 60 k it is a tie at best.

## 4. The limitation, stated before anyone relies on this

The reconstruction is a **flat request**: 42 messages in one turn. A real multi-turn session's floor is larger,
because the keep law protects the first 3 and last 6 *turns* and each turn carries tool mass — the W8 closure
measured a floor of 53,790 on a 12-turn session with a 5,483-token system prompt. **So this card does not prove
a 24 k cell will act on real traffic; it proves the floor is a function of the system prompt and the turn
structure, and gives the arithmetic that makes a budget choice decidable.** The pre-cell check below is what
turns it into evidence.

## 5. What I would freeze for H12

| parameter | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| budget | **24,000** | the only regime where outgoing ≤ 24 k and the price units are 22–37 % of naive (60 k cannot win) |
| profile | terse system prompt (~300 tok), toolsets `[file]`, `agent.max_turns` as in W8 | the flat-request floor is 16.7 k only with the terse prompt |
| arms | `w8cn-{naive,paged}` with `CTXVM_SELECTION_MODE=stable` on the paged arm | the mode is built, tested and live in 0.13.25; the gateway has restarted |
| shape | T-A v2 (20 parts, extraction prompts) — **not T-B**, which is closed | `design/TB-CLOSURE-20261118.md` |
| mode | one pair, `naive` vs `stable`, identical prompts | the design's §4 cell |
| **pre-cell check (free)** | from the first control capture: `turn.render.prefix.approxInputTokens` at late turns must be ≥ ~1.3 × 24,000, and the paged arm's floor must sit below 24,000 | if the control's request never exceeds the budget the cell is a no-op before it is paid for |
| acceptance | the design's four conditions unchanged, condition 2 as **outgoing ≤ 24,000** | parsed-equal artefact, prefix ≤ 24 k, `selectionHash` unchanged between triggers, cache-write below naive with cache-read above |

If the pre-cell check fails, the honest outcome is "no budget separates floor from resident on this shape", and
the cell is not bought — that is cheaper than buying it and recording a no-op.
