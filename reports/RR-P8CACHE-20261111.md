## 2. The verdicts as recorded

**Verdict 1 — the ranking is adopted with one amendment.** (1) watermark compaction (evict once on a
trigger, hold the set) is the candidate, above break-even, and it is the shape the in-tree design card
already specifies (parts 2–4) with a four-condition acceptance gate. (2) **(d) is amended**: it may
gate a *compaction*, but it cannot promise "no worse than control" — the measured no-op arm (0
evictions) cost 4.4× control, so the cost driver is session growth plus output volume, not
selection. A cost gate is still the cheapest first build, but it must gate the COMPACTION (whose cost
is the rewrite), not the absence of selection. (3) (c) is confirmed as "a policy of not evicting" and
cannot bound residency on this shape. The falsifier the reviewer names is adopted verbatim: a run
where the common prefix stays maximal between triggers (byte-compared) and the dollar ratio is still
worse than control kills (1); a byte-preserving no-op that still costs more than control kills (2)
too — **and that second condition is already met by `RR-NOOP`, which is the single most decision-
relevant fact in this card.**

**Verdict 2 — the metric-replacement rule is adopted as a standing rule** (five conditions: the
hypothesis changed; a dated deviation whose reason does not reference the observed direction; new
cells under a new preregistration only, existing cells never re-scored; both metrics with equal
prominence; the symmetry test). Recorded as a standing rule in the lane prompt, so it binds this
lane's future preregistrations too. Existing cells stay under tokens ✓; the replacement run's
preregistration is separate and descriptive ✓; **no cost-saving claim is made anywhere** (law 7).

**Verdict 3 — Q3 is answered by the books' own state, not by a new instruction.** The grid stopped;
W8 closed negative (owner Option A, packet P9); the replacement run is the unpaid-model plan. The
reviewer's remaining instruction — a separate single-cell preregistration of watermark compaction,
run only after the no-op byte check passes and the break-even arithmetic shows the session can pay
back — is **half-satisfied and half-unreachable on the measured shape**: the no-op check passes
(`RR-W8NOOPBYTES`), but the arithmetic cannot pay back (n* ≈ 189 calls at the session-weighted 5.3 %
evictable share vs 5–30 measured calls). So no paid cache-stable cell is justified by the books'
numbers as they stand; the live next step is the owner's: either the free/unpaid replacement run
(descriptive only, no cache prices to break even against) or a different shape (12→20 parts, ~21k
evictable ≈ 23 % of peak at budget 70,000, `design/W8B-FREE-PLAN.md`) — and a routing/TTL question
that the books' `RR-W8CACHE` H3 leaves open.
