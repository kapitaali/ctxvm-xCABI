## The floor is DERIVED, not measured by the diagnostic

The protected set that binds is built at the late turns — system prompt + head 3 + tail 6 — and a
one- or two-prompt diagnostic never reaches them. So:

```
system prompt            4,350 tokens   (measured: turn.render.systemPromptBytes = 17.4 kB)
head 3 turns            14,100          (measured growth ~4,700/turn)
tail 6 turns            28,200
scaffold + schemas       2,000
                        ------
floor                   48,650           DERIVED from the W8B series

budget                  60,000           margin 11,350 ≈ 2.4 turns of growth
```

The diagnostic's job was the other half, and it passed: `faultsBySource` all zero (the text signal is
gone), `proseMentionsTotal = 1` (the observer still sees prose), and `declares` as expected — the pair
is the measurement that tests the floor.

## The eviction volume is a range with a bound

The W8B residency series is a **pinned ceiling**: with 19 pins nothing was ever stubbed, so it is an
upper bound on the new instrument's residency. Crossing 60,000 can therefore happen no earlier than
turn 14, and the six-turn window 14-19 is an upper bound. A narrower window is a finding about the
shape, not a falsification of the change. The falsifier stays decidable: **zero evictions at a
floor+margin budget means the floor is binding, not the signal.**

## Attribution is copied from the run, never re-read from the capture

Measured today: a paged session's capture attested the control arm because the arm fields were
process-global at flush time, and a restart's re-flush rewrote every capture with the then-current
values. 0.12.2 keys them by session, but the rule stands for the record:

- the arm numbers in the card and the LOG row are copied from the probe and the runner's table **at
  run time**;
- the sidecar is corroboration, not the source;
- the verifier is run once, immediately after the arm completes, and its output is kept in the bundle.
