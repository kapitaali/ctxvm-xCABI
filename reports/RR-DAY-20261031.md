## 3. Defects verification found (not planned work)

1. **CI tested less than the mirror claimed to be.** Diffing the workflow's `run:` commands
   against `run-ci.sh` found three gaps: six fixtures (`corpus/m6`, `m7`, `m8`) never validated
   in CI (24 in the mirror vs 18 in the workflow), three drop checksum steps missing entirely
   (CI verified m1–m5 only, so every drop after M5 had no integrity check), and the export
   round-trip chain absent from CI. All three closed.
2. **The mirror could not see its own drift.** `run-ci.sh` executes its own text, so a step that
   exists only in the workflow — or is missing from it — stays invisible to a green local gate.
   That is why the rehearsal tool exists, and why the two surfaces now share one script
   (`tools/export-roundtrip.sh`) for the chain they both run.
3. **A factual error in my own reporting, corrected in writing.** I described npm in the `ts`
   job as a new class of dependency; the job already ran `pip install pytest`. Corrected in
   `RR-W10BUILD` and `design/packaging.md` §9 rather than quietly reworded.
4. **A tool that assumed local state.** The rehearsal looked for PyYAML in `venvs/odps` and
   failed on its first reuse in a repo that has no venvs. It now bootstraps a parser venv.
