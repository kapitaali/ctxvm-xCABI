## Why it evicts nothing: the floor is above the budget

Measured from the capture, not inferred:

```
per-turn request tokens   10,518 → 104,032
crosses 70,000            turn 13 (71,289); turns 14-19 stay above
page.declare              21        (distinct pages 21)
page.fault                19        (re-demanded pages)
page.pin                   0        (no explicit pins; faults carry the pin per the timing table)
evictedTokens             0 on every turn
```

The keep law protects the head (3 turns), the recency tail (6 turns) and every page pinned by a fault.
On this shape 19 of 21 pages are demanded again after their first declaration, so 19 pages are pinned,
and the pinned block plus head plus tail is at or above 70,000 tokens **before anything is eligible for
eviction**. `_plan` therefore hits `floor_tokens >= v_budget` and returns early on every one of the 43
selections — by design (L5), not by defect.

That is the same signature as the W8 twelve-turn runs (zero evictions at 24,000, 28,000 and 55,000),
now with a shape that actually crosses the budget and with the instrument proven to be engaged.

## What this says about the policy, stated carefully

On a read-once workflow where the harness re-sends every tool result on every call, "pin a page when it
is demanded again" is equivalent to "pin nearly everything". The policy cannot distinguish a page that
the model needs again from a page the harness merely re-sends. Its own law then protects the whole
transcript, and no budget below the pinned block can ever bite.

This is a statement about this policy on this shape. It is not a general result about paging.

## Options, and what each costs

**(a) Raise the paged budget above the pinned block.** Measured residency peaks at 104,032; the floor
is above 70,000 and below that. A budget near 90,000 would leave turns 16-19 evictable — a narrow
window, and a new prediction block is required because the budget is part of it.

**(b) Close W8B with this NO-OP as the result.** It is an honest outcome of a working instrument: the
policy has no purchase on a read-once shape because every page is re-demanded and pinned. Five voids
preceded it; this one is not a void.

**(c) Change the policy so faults do not pin re-sent pages.** That is a new policy, a new
preregistration, and a different experiment from the one this card registered.
