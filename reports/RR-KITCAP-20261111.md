  Without the watcher the kit would have stayed red until the next session-start paste; with the

## 3. Why this was not left to the other lane

Two ways to read it: "their commit, their fix" or "the kit cap is a shared law and the kit is red
now". The lane books pick the second: AGENTS §7 says a shared file is claimed by a commit, not a
message, and `tools/check-handoff.sh` must stay green — a red kit blocks every session-start paste,
which is the review round's transport. The compaction precedent is the same shape as the
QUESTIONS.md compaction passes already recorded in the ARCHIVE errata: the index line points, the
full text stays in the owning lane's records, and the change is logged as an erratum rather than
silently applied. The owner should know it happened: the w8b lane's line was the cause and may
want to restore its longer form inside the cap (or move the detail into their card, which is where
it already lives).

## 4. Standing rule added to the lane prompt

**PROJECT-STATE cap:** the file is at 6,123/6,144 B — 21 B of headroom. Any lane adding a line here
must re-run `bash tools/check-handoff.sh` BEFORE committing; a Done-window line can silently push

binds, compact the longest index line to a pointer (keep its card ids) and shave this lane's own
