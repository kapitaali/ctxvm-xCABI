## Against the block — two deviations, both recorded

```
predicted onset      turn 14 or later           observed turn 13             MISSED by one turn (early)
predicted volume     5,000-20,000 tokens         observed 210,856 cumulative  MISSED, 10.5x the upper bound
predicted artefact   PASS parsed-equal           PASS                         HIT
predicted cost       $0.00                      $0.00                        HIT
```

**The volume miss is my specification error, not an instrument failure.** `evictedTokens` counts what the
engine dropped *on that turn*; the frozen middle is re-dropped every turn, so the session figure is a sum
of re-evictions. I wrote 5-20 k as if the counter reported unique content. The review lane reached the
right magnitude before the run (~185 k, from the arms' residency difference) and the right warning —
their number is within 12 % of what the engine reported. Their onset, turn 4, is not: the arms' residency
difference is not an eviction count, and at turn 4 the request was ~30 k against a 60 k budget, so
nothing could have been dropped. The engine's own counter says turn 13.

Rule recorded for the next block: **the volume column must name its unit** — unique content evicted,
per-turn peak, or session sum of re-events — and the unit must match the counter the verifier reads.

## The defect this run exposed, fixed

The control arm's coverage sidecar attested `budgetTokens: 60000, budgetSource: settings` — the paged
arm's value — on a profile that carries no budget, while the trace said 200000/default. Arm, profile,
engine setting and selection counts became session-keyed in 0.12.2; the budget did not. It does now
(`cc8f833`, plugin 0.13.1), with a test that asserts both sides. Attribution above was taken from the
run's output and the trace's own `turn.render` series, not from that sidecar field.
