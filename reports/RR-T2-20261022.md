   they already comply on dataHolder (flat), license (scope/termination/governance) and
   version (quoted string), which sharpens the drift issue to "HTML spec + older examples".

   "already satisfied" with the tool absent — the auditor's bare
   `DATACONTRACT_CLI_NOT_INSTALLED` token is the place a remediation hint belongs.
