## 2. Two physical blockers on `corpus/m1`–`m8` verified by the review lane

`PREREG-E2-DRAFT-20261119.md` §2 proposes:
*"Data: 20 prompts drawn from frozen corpus traces; 10 pages per trace … prompts carry only the task text and the
ablated context"*.

Direct inspection of `corpus/m1`–`m8` proves that even Phase 0 (offline freeze) cannot execute that design on the
frozen corpus:

| blocker | verification command | measured fact |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Traces are content-blind** (no prompt text, no page content) | scan all 17 `corpus/m*/*.jsonl` for `content` / `text` on `page.declare` (`RR-RESVERIFY` check) | **0 of 17 traces** carry page body text or user prompt text. As Part III §4.1 (`research/white-paper-Part-III.md:144`) states: *"A trace holds identities and hashes, not page content."* An ablated context ($\tau \setminus p$) cannot be constructed from `corpus/m1`–`m8` JSONL files alone. |
| **B. Corpus lacks 20 traces × 10 pages (and `tool_recall` pages)** | count `turn.render`, `page.declare`, and unique `logicalRef` per file across `corpus/m*/*.jsonl`, cross-checked with `research/e1-offline/fault-counts.json` | Only **17 traces** exist in `corpus/m1`–`m8` (118 `page.declare` events total), and **only 3 of 17 have $\ge 10$ unique pages**: `m4/hermes-live-session-01.jsonl` (2 turns, 13 unique pages, **1** `tool_recall` fault), `m5/opencode-live-session-01.jsonl` (14 turns, 17 unique pages, **0** `tool_recall` faults — all 17 faults are `identifier_scan`, which E1 scored **negative at 2.4%**), and `m7/hermes-resume-session-b.jsonl` (1 turn, 34 unique pages, **0** faults). Eleven of the remaining 14 traces have $\le 3$ unique pages. |

**Precondition for any future E2 reopen:** a content-bearing prompt + page fixture bank paired with its xCABI
trace (so $\tau$ and $\tau \setminus p$ exist as actual text), stratified on `tool_recall` rather than the negative
`identifier_scan` stratum, plus a fresh owner cost-ceiling sign-off.
