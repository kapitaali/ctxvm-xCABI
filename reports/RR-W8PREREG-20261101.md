## 1. What was done

- **`design/w8-capture-design.md`** — the preregistered capture design, 14 sections
  (§0–§13). Gist (the numbers live there with their locators):
  - **Arms:** `naive` = stock Hermes built-in compressor (never fires at preregistered
    scales; the run must record that it never fired, else the task drops from both
    arms) vs `paged` = a new ctxvm context-engine plugin implementing the v1
    `FaultPinPolicy` law, enforcing the virtual budget `vBudget` (preregistered
    24,000 tokens; the T0 lock may raise it, a lowering is a deviation). One-factor
    design: the ONLY configured difference between arms is the context engine.
  - **Seam (verified at source, the v0.22.5 pin):** `agent/agent_init.py:1904`
    `_select_context_engine()` (`context.engine` → bundled loader → plugin registry
    with `clone_for_agent`); the `ContextEngine` ABC contract
    (`agent/context_engine.py`) read in full — `select_context()` per-request
    resident-set replacement (request-only, runs before cache-control so
    breakpoints re-derive on the selected list), `prune_tool_results_only()`
    (no-op in v1 so the two seams cannot double-apply), `update_from_response()`
    canonical usage buckets INCLUDING `cache_read_tokens`/`cache_write_tokens`,
    and one preregistered validity condition: safe-worker mode must be OFF (it
    silently degrades both arms to builtin). **Verdict recorded in the design:
    the interface FITS implementing FaultPinPolicy v1.**
  - **Task grid:** k = 6 real-repo task templates + mechanical checker scripts,
    R = 3 runs per (task, arm) cell in a prescribed alternating order; texts +
    checkers freeze in the capture commit at T0, before the first run.
  - **Metrics (07-verbatim, reference-pinned in `benchmarks/metrics/metrics.py`):**
    NTR = 1 − paged_net/naive_net over RECORDED arm baselines (the CLI's
    `--baseline` recorded-naive mode; the counterfactual is fence-checked to
    non-gate use), `hard_fault_recall` on the W3 ground truth with recovery as a
    full-render FACT, deterministic bootstrap CIs (seed 20261029, 2000 replicates,
    seed and replicates ride every output).
  - **Gates (07 §M0 rows exact):** NTR ≥ 1.5× with accuracy non-inferior
    (preregistered: paired per-task readout, non-inferiority margin δ = 10 pp,
    one-sided 95% via the paired-task bootstrap); `hard_fault_recall` ≥ 95% at the
    bootstrap 95% CI lower bound with n ≥ 30 hard faults pooled across BOTH arms
    (they occur in both — explicit re-materializations); replay determinism 100%.
  - **Stopping rule:** coverage stop (every cell ≥R = 3 AND n ≥ 30 pooled hard
    faults), continuation is preregistered round-robin across ALL templates (extra
    rounds on the easiest template alone = the forbidden cherry-picking); per-task
    abort when the naive arm's transcript hits 75% of the real window (the design's
    own pressure shortfall, named, not a paging failure); engine-crash abort (4
    total on the paged arm = BLOCKED report); NO mid-run expansion — a defect found
    after runs start turns the runs into a PILOT record and the gate re-runs under
    a new design version.
  - **Budget ledger (D5):** USD 25 hard cap, append-only `reports/W8-SPEND.md`
    opened in the capture commit; the `priceModelVersion` 2 bump with the paid row
    (provider, model, both rates, source URL + access date, `verified`) happens
    BEFORE the first paid run; free-tier rows are legal and leave the ledger at 0
    (tokens stay the primary unit); a binding pre-run projection stop
    (`spent + 2× projected > $25` halts new runs); ledger states est-arithmetic
    USD AND actual billed spend side by side (D5's own words in the decision text).
  - **D12 fork RULED (owner ratifies at the gates):** est* keep page-table
    semantics on every lane; the hermes port gains the `post_api_request` →
    provider-usage wiring as a W8 implementation precondition (the W2b-designated
    seam, `packages/hermes/OBSERVABILITY.md` F3 + §2 note 4; `providerReported`
    flips TRUE in W8 captures — the v0.1 field already exists, node W2b is the
    precedent); W3 pricing reads the est columns and the provider receipt sums ride

  - **Redaction DQ law registered (08.1 §4):** disposable `mkdtemp` workspaces; an
    automated credential/email/home-path/hostname scan over every trace, sidecar,

    implementation card, the law is the preregistration); provenance per m8's
