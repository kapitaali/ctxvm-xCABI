## Driver fix applied

The driver originally passed `-m/--provider/-t` on turn 1 only. That caused
`Resume retains gateway session policy; -m/--model, --provider differ from this session's launch`
on every attempt, because the gateway's stored session policy carries no model/provider and the
client's resume path refuses overrides. Fix: drop `-m/--provider/-t` from all commands and rely on
the profile defaults (gpt-6-luna / nous / hermes-cli toolset). `build_cmd` now takes only the
session name, workspace, and prompt.

## Side finding — 0 evictions (H7 implication)

Every `turn.render` row carries `evictedTokens: 0`. The declared mass (3,758 tokens) is small
because the model shelled out instead of reading; the fixture itself is ~55,909 tokens. With the
surface pinned (defect 1) and parts 01-03 sealed (defect 2), a re-run at a budget in the
48,000-52,000 range (below naive's 59,793 peak, above the 41,700 keep-law floor) is the first
cell whose result means anything. At 24,000 the engine returns early (`floor_tokens >= v_budget`)
regardless of turn count. Consequence: H7's `evictedTokens > 0` gate cannot pass at 24,000 with
this task; the budget must be raised (pre-registered as allowed). Per H8, the honest outcome
pending that cell is a negative result, not a closure.
