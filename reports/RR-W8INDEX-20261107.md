## (e) What the flushed traces prove (all re-derived from `.coverage.json` + events)

1. **The growth curve is real and page-policy-insensitive.** The two pre-restart paid captures'
   per-request size curves are the same shape: ×71.7 and ×69.7 in chars (15,836→1,134,852 and
   15,764→1,098,268 over n=150), last-sample delta 3.3% — whether one of them is the paged arm or
   not is unproven in-trace (the known witness defect), and either way the paired curve shows no
   policy-shaped divergence. The one cell with proven arm attribution (cell A, of the re-scoped
   fixture) grew ×89.2 over its 150 calls. This is the negative evidence the halt is built on:
   paging was INERT where it was declared load-bearing.
2. **estHit vs provider receipts: different quantities, and the ratio is instrument-shaped,**
   consistent with the on-record explanation in RR-W8LIVE ("a different quantity, not a biased
   one"). Per-call estHit/provider-cached ratios ride each trace's `call.meta` events: 0.000
   throughout the luna paid cells (estHit parked at 0 with pages declared), rising late-run toward
   parity on the reflection ghost (0.000→0.764→1.293 over six calls), and 0.000–0.091 on the
   deepseek-family cells. Nothing about pricing should read either as agreement or disagreement
   before W13's receipt-vs-est decision is litigated — this card only pins the observed ranges.
3. **Receipts: the ledger's money is reconcilable against cache-accounting columns.** Corrected
   pre-restart run: cachedInputTokens 21,747,580 vs uncached input 21,989,502 — cached reads priced
   at 98.9% of the uncached-input column (and 49.7% of the billed prompt in+cached when read as a
   share — both readings stated because "cache rate" is ambiguous); cell A: cached 24,016,030 vs
   uncached 24,248,569 and ~232,089 of cache-writes (the LOG row's reconciliation gap). Cell A's
   single `call.meta` receipt rides in 3 / out 51 / cached 231,721 — the turn-boundary absence
   (defect 5) means one receipt event per capture, not the run's sum; sums live in the ledgers.
4. **The search-bound verdict is already in the wire, even without the broken witness.**
   `search_files`-mention counts per trace: 823 (cell A), 714 (the c1-window capture), 995 and 521
   (the pre-restart pair) — against `read_file`'s 14 declares and a recorded 686-of-700 declares
   being search declares (9947bf0's row). Meanwhile the digest-bounded mint path proven in
   RR-STUBPRO (canonicalize + 120-cap + `#sha256:` stamp) fired **zero times across the 24
   traces**: no `#sha256:` stamp occurs in-trace. The hermes harness emits its own raw refs; the
   mint is the node lane's ref builder, so these captures never exercised the bounded path — the
   §12.4 live-transport gap made visible in a paid run, recorded as fact rather than a new defect.
5. **Content-blindness confirmed positive:** no event variant carries model free-text, so the
   analysis that pins the failure to a measured mechanism (150-call ceiling + search-bound declare
   mix) was fully possible with the wire exactly as sealed. The closure question that REQUIRES the
   content channel (per-trace comprehension of the 686 searches) is the content-bearing-capture
   design question the freeze has to own separately (owner-gated, Part II §6.3) — not a W8 closure item.
