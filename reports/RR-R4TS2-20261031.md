## 3. Semantics recorded (same law text as the node card)

- `ts` = the port's own mint instant — front-stream at emit, tail facts at the
  turn's render snapshot (cached, idempotent). Wall-clock provenance, NOT
  latency; monotonicity pinned (RFC 3339 UTC sorts lexicographically in time).
- The opt-out is per-trace (read once at `__init__`); every line stamps or none.
- Timestamps originate from `datetime.now(timezone.utc)`; µs→ms truncation
  (`microsecond // 1000`) — the pattern is ms-only by definition (§R4).
