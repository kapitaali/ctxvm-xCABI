## 3. Deviations from the card

- **The proposal covers all five accumulated motivations, not only umpire's two.**
  W4B B3, W6 §3 and W12 §1 each routed their spec gap to this card; batching them
  under one D4 decision is cheaper than five unsealings. The card's "e.g." is
  satisfied by the R1 mechanism; R2–R5 are explicitly separable.
- **Fixtures live under `design/rfc-0.1/`** rather than a spec directory: they are
  proposal shapes, and the sealed v0 tree must not carry non-conformant bytes.
- **No v0.1 replayer was written**, so "0 paging faults" after R1 is a rule-level
  classification, not a witnessed replay; the proposal says so.

**Verdict: byte-compatible, NOT an independent implementation.** Umpire's
`spec/ledger_tools.py` header declares itself a "Python mirror of
`ctxvm/packages/node/src/ident.ts` @ 361043e". A mirror passing is a conformance
check, not a second implementation; the repo may **not** use the phrase "second
implementation" on this evidence. Scope caveats: ASCII-only per umpire §2, floats
banned, labels sorted-unique by umpire's law.

## 5. Discoveries

1. **Intentional re-executions are indistinguishable from hard faults to every
   source-keyed classifier.** W3's `hard_faults()` counts `tool_recall`; umpire's
   three deliberate rows would enter a gate metric as real hard faults. R1 makes
   the safe classification the default (unlike an `intentional` flag).
2. **A version marker is load-bearing, not cosmetic.** Because v0 is strict,
   v0.1 traces are rejected rather than misread; `session.open.schemaVersion` lets
   a consumer choose the right schema instead of trial-parsing. It also gives W4B
   B4's observer-contract version a natural home.
3. **The clock has three customers** (W6 OTel, W7 RFC-0.1, W12 runtime ABI) and
   the payloads already carry durations (`call.meta.latencyMs`; Hermes
   `api_duration` and `post_tool_call.duration_ms`), so R4 is cheap.
4. **The ref exclusion is correct and should stay** (§3.2): moving pin/seed into
   the ref would turn A/B comparison into separate pages and destroy the property
   umpire measures.
