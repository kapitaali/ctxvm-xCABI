# RR-W8ERRATA-20261110 — C6: errata, instrument defects, and a verdict taxonomy

Zero spend. Committed text is not rewritten; corrections are appended here and cross-referenced.

## 1. Erratum — `RR-W8CELL2-20261110` §4

The committed §4 attributes the cell's cost to "parts appended each turn". That explanation is
**superseded**. Appending writes the new tokens once — a few thousand per call — and the cell wrote
1,353,671 tokens over 29 calls (46.7 k per call) against a growth of 5–9 k per call. Writes are 5–9×
the delta. The cause is **UNKNOWN**; what is known is that it is not the engine's message list (C1:
append-only series, constant system prompt; C2: byte-identical no-op).

coverage sidecar. That single addition would have made C1 answerable and C3 validatable.

## 3. Verdict taxonomy, proposed

Current `VOID` mixes broken data with outcomes, and a NO-OP is therefore read as a failure. Split:

```
DATA-INVALID   the record or the treatment cannot be trusted
               multiple session ids, a shell/code tool used, a SUCCESSFUL sealed-part read,
               stale artefact present at start, budget mismatch, coverage/replay non-zero
               -> does not count, is not evidence of anything

OUTCOME        the record is sound and the treatment ran
               NO-OP        engine ran, evicted nothing (a result about the harness)
               ARTEFACT-FAIL artefact wrong or missing (a result about the model on this task)
               CEILING      iteration cap hit (a result about the task's size)
               CELL         evicted and artefact correct
               -> all three non-CELL outcomes stay in the denominator
```

Rule that follows: **a NO-OP is a result, not a discard.** Today's pair and diagnostic cells are
NO-OPs; the tally "0 cells counted" should read "0 CELL, 3 NO-OP, 1 DATA-INVALID".

## 4. Prompt digest in the resume key

The resume key is `(round, task, arm)` plus a fixture digest (12 hex). It must also carry a **prompt
digest**: two runs of the same task with different prompts (the `widest_file` wording changed today)
are not the same cell. Proposal: sha256 of the 12 prompts + the artefact spec, 12 hex, stamped into
the run directory beside the fixture digest, and compared by `--plan`.

## 5. Why this matters now

Three of the four defects above have already caused a wrong conclusion in this card: the flat render
series, the `inputTokens: 3` field and the missing cache columns. The taxonomy is the cheapest of the
four and prevents the most expensive error — discarding a real negative result as if it were noise.
