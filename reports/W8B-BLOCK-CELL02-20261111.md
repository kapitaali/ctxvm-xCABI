## Why this budget, from measurement rather than arithmetic

Run `w8b-20261111-175250` recorded the per-turn request series on this exact shape:

```
turn   0-11   10,518 → 62,014
turn  12-13   66,784 → 71,289      (crosses 70,000)
turn  14-17   76,034 → 90,609
turn  18-19   95,219 → 104,032     (peak)
```

At 70,000 the floor (pinned pages + 3 head turns + 6 tail turns) was already above the budget, so
`_plan` returned early on all 43 selections. The pinned block cannot shrink between turns, so the only
way to give the policy any room is to put the budget above the floor. 95,219 at turn 18 is the first
turn where the request exceeds 90,000; that is the window this block predicts, and it is narrow on
purpose: a budget high enough to be reachable, low enough that the last two turns must act.

If the verifier shows `evictions == 0` again, the floor is above 90,000 too — that would be a second
measured data point pointing at (c) in `RR-W8B2-RUN2-20261111.md` §Options (the policy cannot tell a
page the model needs from one the harness re-sent), not a reason to raise the budget again.
