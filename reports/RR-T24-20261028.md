## Survey facts the design is built on (probed 2026-10-28)

- The package is **content-blind for FULL pages**: `page.render` carries
  `{page: {logicalRef, contentHash}, mode}` — no text (RFC-0: the tracer
  meters decisions). Resident-page content lives in the app's knowledge
  store → honest materialization REQUIRES an app-supplied resolver; the
  library never invents content (T22 law: the module only orchestrates).
- The package is **self-sufficient for STUBS**: `page.evict` carries
  `stubText` — the exact text the model was told (spec 02-xcabi §table:
  "paging decision + what the model was told").
- The projection is typed and complete: `page-table-N.json` slots
  `{reference, kind, tokens, mode, pinned}` + tally + violations.
- The library's `transformParams` sees `params.prompt` — the exact mutation
  point the design names; the mirror law is what injection must be gated
  against.

## Disclosed

- **No mutation anywhere in v0**: the library's middleware keeps returning
  params unchanged even when inject is armed — the design-letter mutation
  (middleware prepends the brief to `params.prompt` per request) is the
  recorded v-next control shape. The app owns assembly; the tracer stays
  read-only; the brief is never spliced into any stream and never persisted
  (derived data).
- **The stub-vs-full asymmetry is the package's own truth**: stubs inject
  package-completely; full pages render exactly what the app's resolver
  returns — an unresolvable resident page is a LOUD unresolved marker, never
  silently dropped and never thrown.
- **Scope**: single-turn-package briefs (the resume lane's own shape — a
  package holds one slice); no hermes/opencode changes (their injection
  feasibility stays their lane per the design's per-harness fallback);
  no engine/corpus/manifest changes.
