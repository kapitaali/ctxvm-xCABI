binding source is `RR-P3REDACT-20261109` §"D17 and the D6 question": D6 covers **publication and packaging**

release and the external distribution surfaces**, plus the consent/notice rule for non-owner installs. My earlier
explanation blurred the two. This card corrects it; no state-of-record line is rewritten by this card, and the
