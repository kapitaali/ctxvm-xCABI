## Why it matters (recorded, not cosmetic)

Three failure modes were live, not hypothetical:

1. A reader cloning the repo could rebuild the PDF's *claims* but not its *source*.
2. A 7 MB build tree was one `git add -A` away from being committed forever.
3. `.opencode/plugins/ctxvm.ts` re-exports the adapter by absolute path — local state
   shaped like repo content. A future agent editing it would have changed nothing.
