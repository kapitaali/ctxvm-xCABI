ctxvm Trace Corpus

ctxvm-corpus-0.1

A curated, checksummed, versioned collection of deterministic agent-session traces (pages, renders, evictions, faults) plus benchmark suites and golden conformance vectors. Replays byte-identically per the xCABI invariants. Drops: corpus/m1 (seed — three reference/adapter-derived traces plus two tracer-generated sessions), corpus/m2 (first live harness captures — echo and sustained-pressure sessions from a real OpenCode loop on a free-tier model), corpus/m3 (first Hermes plugin traces — synthetic echo and sustained-pressure sessions generated by the Python tracer port), and corpus/m4 (first LIVE Hermes plugin capture — 13 pages, one tool_recall fault and one errored call from a real gateway-backed session at plugin version 0.2.0, paths redacted), and corpus/m5 (first interactive self-capture — a long-running OpenCode TUI agent recording its own working turns: 17 pages, 17 one-turn-late cold-entry faults, 14 turns, zero evictions, flush witnessed at graceful TUI exit), and corpus/m6 (first pause/resume pair — a real OpenCode agent run paused mid-session by the UC1 turn-shaped handoff trigger at turn 3: the packaged snapshot via the committed export binary plus the session booted with CTXVM_RESUME_FROM and the resumeFrom sidecar evidence; 9 pages across both sessions, zero evictions, pause byte-identical to the era-as-flushed).
