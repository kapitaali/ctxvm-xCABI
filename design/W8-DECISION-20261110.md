## What the evidence now says

1. **The session shape caps the effect.** The keep law protects the system prompt, the first three
   turns and the last six. On a 12-turn session that leaves nothing unprotected until turn 9; the
   cumulative evictable ceiling is **25,404 of 479,076 request tokens = 5.3%** (`RR-W8SIM` §2).
2. **A budget cannot fix that.** Eviction needs the budget below floor + middle ≈ **66,600**. The
   planned 72,000 would evict **zero** — the reviewer predicted this and the arithmetic confirms it.
3. **The engine is not rewriting the prefix.** The request series for `w8-cell-01` is strictly
   append-only with a byte-constant system prompt, and a unit test shows the engine's no-op output is
   byte-identical to its input (`RR-W8CACHE` §2, `RR-W8NOOPBYTES`).
4. **The cost is real and unexplained.** 33.5 M cache-write tokens on the day; 46.7 k per call in
   cell-01 against 5–9 k of growth. Cause **UNKNOWN**; the capture has no cache columns and no
   scaffold mass (`RR-W8CACHE` §3).
5. **The cap is smaller than the board says.** ≥ $7.14 billed for one day; store estimate $9.16 over
   three; two days have no export (`RR-SPEND-RECON2`).

## Option A — close W8 with a negative result (recommended)

**Claim the books support:** on a single-turn `-z` session the engine cannot act (granularity); on a
12-turn session at 24,000, 28,000 and 55,000 the engine evicted nothing because the protected floor
plus scaffold exceeds the budget; where the protected set leaves a middle, the session shape caps any
saving at 5.3% of input tokens; in the one measured pair the paged arm's receipt was 3.14× the
control's, and the cause of the extra cache writes is not the engine's message list.

**Not claimed:** that paging is useless in general; any cost saving; any equivalence or
non-inferiority; anything about a different task, model or host.

**Cost:** $0 more. **Go rule:** owner accepts that the paper's W8 section is a negative result plus a
design note on cache-stable selection.

## Option B — one bounded mechanism test

**Preconditions (both must hold):** the eviction budget is below ~66,600 (60,000 is the only value
that leaves a middle and still clears the floor), and the prediction block is committed first.

**Cells:** 4–6 pairs, `w8-cell-01` shape, prompt digest recorded, 12-call ceiling, `-t file`.

**Prediction to be committed before the first call:** at 60,000, evictions on turns 9–11 only,
totalling ≤ 12,827 tokens (17% of the final request, 5.3% of session input); artefact parsed-equal in
both arms; paged receipt ≥ control's. **A mismatch means the instrument is wrong, not the engine.**

**Cost:** $2.2–$3.0 at the measured rate, plus unknown overshoot.

**What it could support:** "the engine evicts under budget with the artefact unchanged, in n of n
pairs; the receipt was not reduced." **Not** a saving claim, and not a 10-point non-inferiority gate —
that needs ~30 pairs with zero failures (`RR-W8GRID` §3).

**Go rule:** owner accepts that the result will be descriptive and that the cache cause stays UNKNOWN.

## Recommendation

**A now, B never — unless the owner wants the mechanism demonstrated.** The mechanism is already
demonstrated at unit level (C2) and the shape cannot carry a saving; B would buy a sentence, at the
price of the remaining cap and another instrument risk. If B is chosen, it must be at 60,000, not
72,000, and the prediction block is non-negotiable.
