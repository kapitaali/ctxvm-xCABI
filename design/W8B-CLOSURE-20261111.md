# W8B — CLOSED: the fault-pin policy has no purchase on a read-once shape

Date: 2026-11-11. Supersedes the open state in `reports/W8B-BLOCK-CELL02-20261111.md`.

## Outcome

**Negative result, measured twice, with an instrument proven to engage.**

```
budget 70,000   paged 41 calls, 43 selections, 0 evictions, parsed-equal artefact   NO-OP
budget 90,000   paged 41 calls, 43 selections, 0 evictions, parsed-equal artefact   NO-OP
                naive 41 calls,  0 selections, 0 evictions                          NO-OP
```

At 90,000 the arms are the same arm: per-turn requests within 1 % of each other
(`10,481 → 103,262` vs `11,030 → 102,204`) and provider receipts within 0.2 %
(input 181,963 vs 181,632; cached 2,132,608 vs 2,143,872). With zero evictions the engine returns
`None` on every call, so the request is unchanged — the receipts confirm the law rather than the
absence of a treatment.

Residency peaks at 102-104 k. The two budgets bracket the protected floor at 90-104 k, so any budget
that could bite leaves at most ~14 k tokens on the last two turns of a 20-turn session: at most ~7 %
of session input, and only once the session is nearly over.

## Why the policy cannot act on this shape

```
page.declare   21 distinct pages
page.fault     19            every part is demanded again after its first declaration
```

The keep law protects the head (3 turns), the recency tail (6 turns) and every page pinned by a fault.
Nineteen of twenty-one pages fault, so nineteen are pinned, and the pinned block alone meets the
budget before anything is eligible for eviction. `_plan` returns early on every selection, by design
(L5).

In a read-once workflow the harness re-sends every tool result on every call; the policy reads that
re-send as a demand and pins the page. **"Pin on re-demand" is equivalent to "pin nearly everything"
when the harness is the one re-demanding.** The policy cannot tell a page the model needs again from a
page the harness merely re-sent.

This is a statement about this policy on this shape. It is not a general result about paging, and it
says nothing about the external controller in arXiv:2610.22732v1.

## What it cost

$0.00. Both subjects were free-tier. Cells booked: none — five voids, then two NO-OPs at two budgets.

## What was fixed on the way (and stays fixed)

```
plugin 0.11.1   per-session arm policy: the arm is resolved from the session's home at call time,
                not from a registration-time snapshot (which made the control run the treatment)
                per-profile selection counters, so a control sidecar cannot report the treatment's
runner          --budget drives preflight, driver and verifier together; tokens come from the
                capture receipts, not the session row (which disagreed by 1.87x on identical work);
                a timed-out CLI is SIGTERMed so the session flushes instead of orphaning
probe           --arm both answers the only question that matters before a pair: CONTRAST OK,
                CONTROL OK for the naive arm, READY for the paged one
profiles        exactly two, enforced by preflight and by tools/w8b-profiles.sh; nothing provisions
                a pair any more (that branch created eight profiles and 368 MB of dead state)
```

## What a cell would require

A policy that does not pin a page merely because the harness re-sent it — for example one that pins on
a model-initiated re-read rather than a request-level repeat, or one that evicts from the tail of the
pinned block by age. That is a different policy and a different experiment: it needs its own
preregistration, its own prediction block, and its own pair of arms.

## Residual state

The two profiles `w8bn-naive` and `w8bn-paged` are deleted with this closure. Their exact configs are
recorded here so a future card can recreate them without re-deriving anything:

```yaml
# both:
model: {default: "meituan/longcat-2.5:free", provider: "nous"}
toolsets: [file]
agent: {max_turns: 6}
plugins: {enabled: [ctxvm-tracer]}
# naive only:
context: {engine: "compressor"}
# paged only:
context: {engine: "ctxvm"}
plugins: {enabled: [ctxvm-tracer], entries: {ctxvm-tracer: {settings: {budgetTokens: 90000}}}}
```

The fixtures, driver, checker, runner and probe stay in the tree: they are the instrument, and the
next card starts from a working one rather than from a hypothesis.

## Claim ceiling

Supportable: "at budgets 70,000 and 90,000, one pair each on `meituan/longcat-2.5:free`, the fault-pin
policy selected 43 times, evicted 0 tokens, and produced requests and receipts indistinguishable from
the control's within 0.5 %, with a parsed-equal artefact."

Not supportable: any saving; any generalisation to another shape, model or policy; any claim that
paging does not work; any claim about model safety.
